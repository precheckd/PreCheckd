## 2. What PreCheckd is

PreCheckd is a hiring site for technical and IT roles where both recruiters and candidates are identity-verified. Recruiters can search candidates by verified skills and levels. Candidates can browse jobs and know that the recruiter behind a job is a verified person. Candidates prove their skills with tests that run from Basic to Expert.

Who can join:
- You must be at least 18 years old.
- Recruiters must be based in the United States and have a US phone number.
- Candidates: for now, only US government-issued IDs can be verified.

## 3. Candidate verification

- Steps: confirm your email, confirm your phone number by text message, then complete an identity check.
- The identity check uses a government-issued photo ID and a live selfie, processed by our verification partner, Stripe Identity.
- Recruiters do not see your ID documents.
- Time: usually a few minutes. If a check needs extra review it can take longer.
- If your phone or ID check gets stuck, email hello@precheckd.com.
- Jobs and degrees: candidates do not have to request this. When a work or education entry is added to a profile, it goes into our verification process. We first try to verify it ourselves with the employer or school, which takes about a week. If that doesn't work, we open a case with the candidate to ask for their help, such as documents. We do not publish an employer or school name on the public verification page.
- After completing the ID check you are a verified member. A public verification page is optional and shows first name and last initial only.

## 4. Recruiter verification

- Steps: sign up with a corporate email and basic information, verify identity with a government ID and a selfie, then go live. Identity verification usually takes about 24 hours.
- Recruiters are checked on identity, phone, email, and company domain.
- A verified recruiter has a public profile with a verified badge.

## 5. Skill tests and levels

- Each skill has up to four tests: Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, and Expert. You start at Basic. Passing a test unlocks the next one.
- A test is passed with at least 80% of its questions right. Tests draw from a question bank, so attempts are not identical.
- The Expert exam is longer and has a much higher pass line: 19 of 20 correct earns Master, and all 20 correct earns Grandmaster.
- Levels shown on a profile:
  - Foundational: you handle the core, entry-level questions.
  - Intermediate: you handle everyday, working-level questions.
  - Advanced: you handle working-level questions and can solve some of the hard ones.
  - Master: you passed the Expert exam.
  - Grandmaster: you answered every Expert question correctly. It is a badge on top of Master.
- Developing and Provisional are shown only to the candidate. Recruiters never see them. Recruiters never see a percentage for a skill test.
- Retakes: once every 60 days for each test, and 90 days for the Expert exam. The latest published result counts.
- Results older than 12 months are shown to recruiters as needing a retake.
- You choose which skills recruiters can see. You can also choose to show how many questions you got right by difficulty. That is off unless you turn it on.
- Integrity: our test provider checks each attempt and can flag suspected cheating or AI use. A flagged attempt is held and a person at PreCheckd reviews it before anyone sees it. A flag alone is not a finding.
- What we keep: your level or score and which questions were right or wrong. We do not keep the question text or your answers. The test provider receives an anonymous ID, not your name or email.
- Don't see your skill? Request it on the Skills page.
- Some tests are built around a job title instead of a single skill. They show an exact score, do not change your skill levels, and appear on your profile only if you choose.
- A recruiter can ask a connected candidate to take a test. The candidate has 72 hours to accept or decline. If accepted, the exact score goes to that recruiter.
- More detail: /skills-explained.

## 6. Jobs and applying

- Anyone can browse jobs. Applying requires being fully verified (email, phone, and identity).
- Each job lists up to three skills with a minimum level. A job can also ask for education, certifications, a security clearance, a shift, a work area, or a start date.
- If a candidate does not meet a requirement, the Apply button stays hidden and the job page says what is missing and links to the fix. When all requirements are met, Apply turns on.
- The Jobs page has tabs: Jobs for you, In my work area, and All open jobs.
- Postings have a pay range, benefits information, and an application close date. After the close date the recruiter must record the outcome.
- Job titles come from a fixed list, not free text.

## 7. Privacy: who sees what

- Recruiters see candidates in anonymized form until a candidate accepts a connection.
- Email address and phone number are not shown on your profile. Contact details are shared with another member only after you connect and choose to share them.
- You can end a connection at any time.
- We never sell your information and do not share it with third parties for marketing.
- Messages sent through PreCheckd are stored and automatically scanned for spam, scams, and malware.
- Service providers: Stripe (ID verification and payments), AWS (text messages and file storage), MongoDB Atlas (database), Resend (email), Render (hosting), CoderByte (tests), Anthropic (AI, for resume reading and this assistant), Google Analytics. Credly only if you link it. Questions typed into this assistant are stored by PreCheckd for up to 90 days to improve it, so do not type personal or sensitive information.
- You can ask for account deletion by emailing hello@precheckd.com. Some limited records may be kept for security, fraud prevention, or legal reasons.
- Full details are in the Privacy Policy (/privacy) and Terms (/terms).
